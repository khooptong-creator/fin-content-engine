## Video direction
The visual tone is modern, sleek, and high-contrast, using deep navy blue backgrounds paired with bright neon crimson and energetic emerald green accents. Typography features bold, clean sans-serif fonts with dynamic text pop-ups to highlight key numbers. Motion design should feel snappy and motion-graphics heavy, using clear visual metaphors like shrinking stacks of cash and compounding clock animations to make financial math instantly visual and engaging for modern audiences.

## Frame 1 - Hook
- duration: 4s
- scene: A sleek credit card floating against a dark background, glowing green, which suddenly turns bright red as an endless spiral wraps around it.
- voiceover: "Paying only the minimum on your credit card bill feels like a cheat code, but it is actually a fifteen-year debt trap."

**Shot Sequence:**
- 0.0s: A glowing credit card appears with a green checkmark showing a Rs 2,500 minimum payment.
- 1.8s: The checkmark turns into a red warning sign as an endless timeline ribbon spirals around the card.

## Frame 2 - The Hidden Math
- duration: 5s
- scene: A graphic of a Rs 50,000 bill splitting into two uneven sections showing interest versus actual debt.
- voiceover: "When you owe fifty thousand rupees, your small minimum payment mostly covers interest while barely touching your actual balance."

**Shot Sequence:**
- 0.0s: A digital tag showing Rs 50,000 breaks apart into a large red block and a tiny green block.
- 2.5s: Labels pop up showing 90% of the payment going straight to interest and only 10% reducing principal.

## Frame 3 - The Time Trap
- duration: 5s
- scene: A digital calendar rapidly flipping through fifteen years while a stack of money barely shrinks.
- voiceover: "Because high credit card interest compounds every month, clearing that original balance will drag on for over a decade and a half."

**Shot Sequence:**
- 0.0s: A calendar fast-forwards through years while a clock spins rapidly in the background.
- 2.5s: The year counter stops 15 years in the future with 80% of the debt visual still remaining.

## Frame 4 - The Real Cost
- duration: 5s
- scene: A Rs 50,000 price tag dramatically multiplying on screen into a massive red total.
- voiceover: "By the time you finally clear the bill, that fifty thousand rupee purchase will end up costing you triple the original price."

**Shot Sequence:**
- 0.0s: The number Rs 50,000 appears center screen in bold yellow text.
- 2.5s: It rapidly ticks upward, flashing bright red as it locks in at over Rs 1,500,000 total paid.

## Frame 5 - The Smart Way Out
- duration: 4s
- scene: A mobile banking app screen toggling a switch from Minimum Payment to Full Payment, turning the interface green.
- voiceover: "Always pay your full statement balance every single month to keep your money in your own pocket."

**Shot Sequence:**
- 0.0s: A finger taps a toggle switch on a modern banking interface changing "Minimum Due" to "Total Due".
- 2.0s: The red warning border dissolves into a clean, bright green success shield with a zero interest badge.
