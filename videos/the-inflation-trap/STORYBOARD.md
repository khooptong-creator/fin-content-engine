## Video direction

**Tone**: Cheerful, childlike, sticker-sheet kawaii.
**Color**: Use `daisy-days` preset (cream, turquoise, soft-pink, mint, butter, peach). Use `coral` for accents only. 
**Design**: Every elevated shape has a 3px charcoal outline and hard offset shadow. Use `Fredoka` for display text and `Quicksand` for body text. Add 3-7 SVG ornaments (stars, daisies, clouds) to every frame, cropping past the edges. No square corners.
